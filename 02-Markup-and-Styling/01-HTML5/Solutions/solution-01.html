Simple Weeknight Lasagna

A quick baked lasagna using no-boil noodles, ready in about an hour.

Ingredients

  • 9 no-boil lasagna noodles
  • 500g ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 3 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Mix ricotta, egg, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  3. Spread a thin layer of marinara in a baking dish.
  4. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, marinara, and mozzarella; repeat three times.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 15 more minutes until golden.
  6. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
